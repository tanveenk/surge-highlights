🌱 Pillar 3: Team Leadership

Fostering Industry-Ready Student Developers

Surge's tech team should be the best learning environment at SFU. Here is how I will lead, mentor, and keep volunteer developers engaged.

● Agile Sprints with Exam Buffers

Running 1-2 week lightweight sprint cycles on GitHub Projects with clear issue ownership. Planning ahead for midterm and final exam seasons so deadlines are never compromised.

● Empathetic & Educational Code Reviews

PR reviews that explain the "why" behind patterns (e.g., SSR hydration, security, clean code). 15-minute weekly pairing sessions for blocked junior engineers.

● Design & Exec Team Collaboration

Weekly syncs with Design and Marketing leads to review Figma mockups early, negotiate technical feasibility, and agree on phased MVPs.

● Resume-Defining Project Ownership

Giving every developer high-visibility ownership over meaningful features (e.g., judging portal, interactive schedule, Discord bot integration).
